Job Positions

Description

We are seeking a skilled and detail-oriented painter to join our dedicated team for permanent, full-time positions. The ideal candidate will have proven experience in residential painting and the ability to deliver high-quality results on every project. If you take pride in your craft, enjoy working both independently and as part of a team, and are committed to excellence, we’d love to hear from you.

Job Details
  • Job Type: Full-time
  • Location: In Person
  • Pay: $18-$20 hourly fee
Qualifications
  • Proven professional painting experience in residential settings
  • Knowledge of surface preparation and painting techniques
  • Experience with interior and exterior projects
  • Proficiency with standard painting tools and equipment
  • Drywall experience
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality
  • Understanding of on-site safety protocols
  • Physical ability to work on ladders and stand for long periods
  • Valid driver’s license and reliable transportation
Responsabilities
  • Prepare, prime, and paint surfaces for residential and light commercial projects
  • Handle both interior and exterior painting with precision and care
  • Maintain a clean and safe job site while following company safety standards
  • Work collaboratively with team members and communicate effectively with clients
  • Ensure quality control and timely completion of all assigned tasks
  • Apply primers, sealers, and paints to interior and exterior surfaces
  • Mix and match paints to achieve the correct colors
  • Use brushes, rollers, and sprayers for various applications
  • Set up scaffolding and ensure that you have covered all fixtures before painting
  • Protect non-paintable surfaces with coverings and tape
  • Perform detailed finish work on trim, edges, and cabinets
  • Maintain tools and equipment in good condition
  • Follow all safety protocols for ladders, scaffolding, and materials
  • Keep the worksite clean and organized daily
  • Arrive on time and adhere to project schedules, ensuring timely completion of work
  • Comply with OSHA regulations

Description

We are currently seeking highly skilled and reliable Tilers/Installers to join our dedicated team for permanent, full-time positions. The ideal candidate will have proven experience in tile installation across residential projects and the ability to deliver precise, high-quality finishes. If you take pride in your craftsmanship, work well both independently and as part of a team, and are committed to excellence, we’d love to hear from you.

Qualifications
  • Proven professional experience in residential tile installation.
  • Knowledge of substrate preparation and installation techniques.
  • Proficiency with tile tools (wet saws, trowels, floats).
  • Familiarity with different types of tiles and flooring (ceramic, porcelain, stone, etc.)
  • Ability to read layouts and perform precise cuts.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Understanding of on-site safety protocols.
  • Reliable transportation and punctuality.
  • Physical ability to lift heavy materials and kneel for extended periods.
  • Expertise in troweling and providing the correct pitch for drainage.
  • Must have a valid driver’s license and reliable transportation to travel to various job sites.
  • Meticulous eye for detail.
Responsabilities
  • Measure, cut, and shape tile to fit around obstacles and designated patterns.
  • Prepare interior substrates (walls, floors, showers) for installation.
  • Apply mortars, adhesives, grouts, and sealants.
  • Install tile in bathrooms, kitchens, and other living areas
  • Ensure precise alignment, spacing, and finishing.
  • Repair or replace existing tile and underlying materials as needed.
  • Maintain tools and equipment in clean, working order.
  • Follow all safety protocols for tools and materials.
  • Keep the worksite clean, organized, and safe.
  • Arrive on time and adhere to project schedules, ensuring timely completion of work.
  • Comply with OSHA regulations
Job Details
  • Job Type: Full-time
  • Location: In Person
  • Pay: $18-$20 hourly fee
